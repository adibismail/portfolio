About Me

I'm a software engineer and project manager based in Malaysia, currently at MDT Innovations. For the past 7 years I've been building systems that sit at the intersection of software, hardware, and real-world operations — think RFID tracking across a university library, IoT sensors on a factory floor, or asset monitoring at one of Southeast Asia's busiest airports.

What I enjoy most is the messy, hard part: getting a sensor talking to a server, getting that server talking to an ERP, and making sure the whole thing actually works reliably in production. I'm equally comfortable writing backend code, configuring cloud infrastructure, or sitting with a client to figure out what they actually need.

AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner. Hands-on with Docker, Linux, RFID, IoT, and full-stack development from day one to go-live.
