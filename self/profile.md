→ **Backend Application**  
→ **Database**  
→ **Dashboard / Business Application**  
→ **ERP / Enterprise System**

I am particularly interested in projects involving **smart factories, industrial IoT, production monitoring, asset and material tracking, traceability, automation, machine-to-system integration, and digital transformation**.
